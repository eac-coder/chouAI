What is chouAI?

It adapts its response depth to your plan: short and direct on Free, full teacher-style explanations on Premium.

FAQ

Common questions

Honest answers to what students actually ask.

What exactly is chouAI? ∨
chouAI is an AI study companion built specifically for students. You type in a math problem or homework question and it gives you a clear explanation — not just the answer. It's not a general-purpose chatbot; it's focused on helping you actually learn.
Is it free? ∨
Yes — there's a free plan that gives you 15 messages per day with short, direct answers. No sign-up required. If you want deeper, teacher-style explanations, the Premium plan is $10/month and gives you 200 messages per day.
How does the AI help with math? ∨
You paste or type your problem, and chouAI explains the method clearly. On Premium, it walks you through the reasoning step by step in a calm, encouraging tone — so you understand how to solve similar problems on your own, not just this one.
Is it accurate? ∨
chouAI is quite reliable for standard math topics like algebra, calculus, and geometry. That said, AI can make mistakes — especially on very complex or ambiguous problems. Always verify important answers, and use it as a learning aid, not a replacement for your teacher.
What's the difference between Free and Premium? ∨
Free gives you 15 short, direct answers per day — great for quick checks. Premium gives you full, detailed explanations with reasoning and context, plus 200 messages per day. Think of Free as a quick hint, Premium as a patient tutor.
